GPS Coordinate Tools Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Version: 2.0.0

We take your privacy very seriously. This privacy policy explains how GPS Coordinate Tools processes your data. All data in this app is processed on-device and stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your data to our servers or any third-party servers.

1. Data We Process

1.1 Data You Actively Provide

When you use GPS Coordinate Tools, you may actively provide the following information, which is stored only on your device:

  • Location Coordinate Information: Latitude and longitude coordinates you manually input, address information, search keywords
  • Calculation Data: Measurement data, calculation parameters, and unit settings you input
  • Favorites and Notes: Location names, note information, and custom tags you save
  • Feedback Information: Feedback and suggestions you provide through customer service channels

1.2 Data Accessed On-Device (Not Collected or Transmitted)

To provide core functionality, the app accesses the following data on your device when you use the relevant features. This data is processed in memory or stored locally on your device only, and is NEVER transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers:

Data Type Access Method Purpose Storage Location
Precise Location CoreLocation (with your permission) Provide GPS coordinate services, distance calculations, and mapping features Memory only - not persisted, not transmitted
Environmental Sensor Data CoreMotion (barometer, magnetometer) Display magnetic field strength, barometric pressure, altitude and other environmental data Memory only - not persisted, not transmitted

Important: This app does NOT collect device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), device model, OS version, app version, usage statistics, crash logs, or any other device information. This app does NOT collect or transmit map interaction data (pan/zoom). All data you see in the app stays on your device.

1.3 Permission Requests

To provide complete functionality, the app requests the following permissions:

  • Location Permission (When In Use, optional): Used to obtain your current GPS coordinates for map display and measurement functions. You can decline this permission; other features remain fully functional.
  • Motion Permission (optional): Used to access barometer and magnetometer for environmental data. You can decline this permission.

Important: Permission requests follow the principle of minimum necessity. You can enable or disable these permissions at any time in your device Settings. Disabling permissions only affects related functions; all other app features continue to work normally.

2. Data Usage

2.1 On-Device Processing Purposes

All data is processed on your device for the following purposes:

  • Map Display: Display your location, marker points, and measurement areas on the map (via Apple MapKit)
  • Location Service: Obtain and display your current location coordinates (on-device only)
  • Distance Measurement: Calculate the distance between points based on your location and map clicks
  • Area Measurement: Calculate the area of an enclosed region based on vertices you select
  • Coordinate Conversion: Convert coordinates between different formats (DD, DMS, UTM, MGRS, etc.) - all calculations are on-device
  • Search Service: Address and POI search via Apple MapKit search - your search queries are sent directly to Apple under Apple privacy policy, not stored by us
  • Environmental Monitoring: Display barometric pressure, magnetic field and other environmental data (on-device only)
  • Favorites: Save your marked locations and calculation results for subsequent viewing
  • History Records: Save your calculation history for reuse and reference

2.2 Usage Restrictions

We promise:

  • We will not use your data for purposes unrelated to providing app functionality
  • We will not sell, rent, or otherwise provide your data to third parties for commercial purposes
  • We will not use your data for personalized advertising
  • We will not transmit your data to any server controlled by us

3. Data Protection

3.1 Local Storage Security

  • Local Storage: Your favorites, history records, and settings are stored locally on your device, protected by iOS sandbox isolation
  • No Server Transmission: This app does not transmit any of your personal data to servers controlled by us. The only network requests are to Apple MapKit for map tiles and search, which are governed by Apple privacy policy
  • HTTPS Encryption: All network communication with Apple services uses HTTPS encrypted protocol

3.2 Data Security Measures

We adopt the following security measures to protect your data:

  • iOS sandbox isolation prevents app data from being accessed by other apps
  • HTTPS encrypted protocol for all network communication
  • Minimum data access - only data necessary for app functionality
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs integrated

4. Your Rights (GDPR and CCPA)

4.1 GDPR Data Subject Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): View all your stored data within the app (favorites, history, settings)
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Edit any saved favorites, notes, or settings within the app
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Delete individual favorites/history items, or use Clear All Data in Settings to delete everything at once
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): Disable location and motion permissions in device Settings to stop data access
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Use the Export My Data feature in Settings to export all your favorites and history to a file
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Uninstall the app or withdraw consent in Settings at any time
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)): Withdraw your privacy consent at any time via Settings - Privacy - Withdraw Consent. This will clear all local data and reset the app

4.2 Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your data based on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For location and motion sensor access - you grant permission and can withdraw it at any time
  • Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): For processing necessary to provide the app functionality you requested
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): For storing your favorites and history on-device to provide the core app experience

4.3 CCPA Notice (California Consumers)

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the personal information we collect about you (we do not collect personal information that leaves your device)
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information (uninstall the app or use Clear All Data in Settings)
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do NOT sell your personal information, so no opt-out is necessary
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: You will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your rights

Do Not Sell or Share: We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration. No opt-out link is required because we do not engage in such practices.

4.4 Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Favorites, history, and settings are retained on your device until you manually delete them or uninstall the app
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the app automatically deletes all locally stored data
  • Clear Data: Use Settings - Clear All Data to delete all local storage at once
  • Memory-Only Data: Real-time location and sensor data is only kept in memory while the feature is in use, and is never persisted to disk

GPS Coordinate Tools uses Apple MapKit for the following functions:

When you use these features, Apple may process your search query or map request under Apple own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not have access to this data. Apple processing is governed by Apple privacy practices.
